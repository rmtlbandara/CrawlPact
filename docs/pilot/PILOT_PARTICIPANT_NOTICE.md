# Pilot Participant Notice

Status: current-authoritative. This is the notice text a Super Admin should share (manually,
outside the product — e.g. in the same conversation used to recruit the participant) before or
when adding someone to a pilot cohort. There is no dedicated public `/pilot/*` join surface in
this phase (§40 — only build one if manual association proves impractical; it hasn't been
needed), so this notice is delivered as plain text/conversation, not a web page.

---

> **You're being invited to an early CrawlPact customer pilot.**
>
> This means:
>
> - You'll be using the normal, real CrawlPact product — not a special demo or a locked-down
>   preview.
> - Your product activity (audits, saved domains, monitoring, etc.) is measured the same way it
>   is for any CrawlPact customer, under CrawlPact's existing privacy policy.
> - You may be asked for optional, structured feedback inside the app (a "Pilot feedback" link).
>   Giving feedback is entirely voluntary.
> - Being part of this pilot does not imply any discount, free plan, or other benefit unless we
>   tell you so explicitly and separately.
> - You can stop participating in the pilot at any time — just tell us. Your CrawlPact account
>   and any data in it are governed by CrawlPact's normal account and privacy policies regardless
>   of pilot participation.
> - Normal support/contact channels apply — reach us at `support@crawlpact.com`.

---

Do not present this as a legally binding agreement — it is a plain-language courtesy notice, not
a substitute for CrawlPact's actual Terms/Privacy Policy, which continue to govern the account.
