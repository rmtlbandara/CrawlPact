## Assets deliberately not treated as authority assets

- `/pricing/`, `/audit/`, `/sign-in` — commercial/functional pages, not reference material; per
  Section 18, outreach effort should not target these directly.
- `/shared/[token]` reports — intentionally private/noindex; see
  `PRODUCT_LED_DISTRIBUTION_REVIEW.md` for their (different) role as brand-awareness touchpoints,
  not public authority assets.

## Headline conclusion

The single highest-value, currently-unrealized authority asset is the **Registry Observatory research
publication workflow** — real infrastructure, zero output. Every other asset in this table already
exists and is already public; the research publication is the one genuine "build/ship" opportunity
Phase 23 identified, and it requires human Super Admin action to actually publish (see
`RESEARCH_AUTHORITY_PLAN.md`).
