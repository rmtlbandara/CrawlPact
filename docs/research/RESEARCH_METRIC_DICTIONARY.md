# Research metric dictionary

Every Registry Observatory metric currently published, as computed by
`apps/web/src/lib/observatory/registry-observatory.ts`. Only Layer A metrics exist this phase —
see `PHASE_16_RESEARCH_CORPUS_DECISION.md` for Layer B.

| Key                                                 | Name                                                | Definition                                                                                    | Numerator               | Denominator                  | Exclusions                                           | Unavailable handling                                                        | Interpretation                                 | Prohibited interpretation                                                              | Comparability                                                                                     |
| --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `crawlerCount`                                      | Tracked crawler records                             | Every crawler entry in the pinned release's snapshot                                          | —                       | —                            | None                                                 | n/a (always populated)                                                      | "CrawlPact currently tracks N crawler records" | Not "N crawlers exist on the internet"                                                 | Comparable across releases; a taxonomy change is called out, not hidden                           |
| `operatorCount`                                     | Distinct operators                                  | Distinct `operatorId` values across the release                                               | —                       | —                            | None                                                 | n/a                                                                         | "N operators are represented"                  | Not an operator-transparency ranking                                                   | Comparable across releases                                                                        |
| `evaluationEligibleCount`                           | Evaluation-eligible records                         | Records with lifecycle `active`, `deprecated`, or `replaced`                                  | evaluationEligibleCount | crawlerCount                 | `unverified`/`retired` excluded                      | n/a                                                                         | Share of registry actively used in evaluation  | Not "share of the web actively crawled"                                                | Comparable across releases                                                                        |
| `purposeDistribution[p]`                            | Records by purpose                                  | Count of records with `purpose === p`                                                         | count                   | crawlerCount                 | None                                                 | n/a                                                                         | "N of M records classified as {purpose}"       | Not "N% of AI crawlers are {purpose}" (this is CrawlPact's registry, not the industry) | Comparable if purpose taxonomy unchanged between releases                                         |
| `lifecycleDistribution[s]`                          | Records by lifecycle                                | Count of records with `lifecycleStatus === s`                                                 | count                   | crawlerCount                 | None                                                 | n/a                                                                         | Registry health/turnover snapshot              | Not a claim about crawler activity in the wild                                         | Comparable across releases                                                                        |
| `verification.verifiedCount`                        | Records with a verification date                    | Count with non-null `lastVerifiedAt`                                                          | verifiedCount           | crawlerCount                 | None                                                 | n/a                                                                         | Source-verification coverage                   | Not "N records are currently accurate" (verification is a point-in-time check)         | Comparable across releases                                                                        |
| `verification.reviewDueCount`                       | Evaluation-eligible records due for re-verification | Eligible records never verified, or verified >180 days ago                                    | reviewDueCount          | evaluationEligibleCount      | Non-eligible lifecycle excluded                      | n/a                                                                         | Registry maintenance-freshness signal          | Not "N records are wrong"                                                              | Comparable across releases (same 180-day threshold)                                               |
| `releaseHistory[i].added/removed`                   | Crawler additions/removals per release              | Semantic-diff added/removed crawler IDs vs the immediately previous published release         | count                   | — (a raw count, not a ratio) | None                                                 | Genesis release has no "previous" — treated as 100% additions, not compared | Registry evolution over time                   | Not a claim about the crawler ecosystem's growth                                       | Only directly comparable release-to-release; not summed across a taxonomy change without a caveat |
| `releaseHistory[i].purposeChanges` / `tokenChanges` | Field-level reclassifications per release           | Count of changed entries whose semantic diff touched that field                               | count                   | —                            | Only entries present in both compared releases       | n/a                                                                         | Registry correction/reclassification activity  | Not evidence of operator crawler behaviour change                                      | Release-to-release only                                                                           |
| `releaseHistory[i].evidenceOnlyChanges`             | Evidence-only corrections per release               | Changed entries where every field change is class `evidence` (source URL, verification dates) | count                   | —                            | Entries with any evaluation-semantic change excluded | n/a                                                                         | "N corrections had no effect on evaluation"    | Not "N crawlers changed behaviour"                                                     | Release-to-release only                                                                           |

All metrics are exhaustive population counts over the pinned release, not a sample — no confidence
interval or margin of error applies. See `RESEARCH_METHODOLOGY.md` for versioning/comparability
rules and `RESEARCH_CLAIM_CLASSIFICATION.md` for how these feed publication text.
