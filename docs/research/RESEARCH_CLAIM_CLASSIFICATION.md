# Research claim classification

Every statement in a Policy Observatory publication falls into exactly one of these classes.

## Observed

Directly measured. Example: "8 of 23 tracked crawler records in registry release 2026.07.3 are
classified with purpose Search." Every `keyFindings` entry in
`apps/web/src/lib/research/publication-content.ts` is an Observed claim, carrying its own
numerator, denominator, and scope.

## Computed

Deterministically calculated from observed data. Example: a percentage derived from an Observed
numerator/denominator pair. Always generated by code (`buildRegistryLandscapeContent`), never
hand-typed into an article body (§52).

## Supported interpretation

An interpretation consistent with the measured signals, stated as such — e.g. "the most recent
published release recorded 2 crawler additions and 1 purpose reclassification relative to the
previous release." Confined to the `sections` (Results/Dataset/Methodology) text, never presented
as a bare number without its basis.

## Hypothesis

A potential explanation requiring more evidence than the current dataset provides. Not currently
used by the Registry Landscape publication (its findings are all directly Observed/Computed) —
reserved for a future Layer B study where a genuine interpretive gap might exist.

## Unsupported

Must not be published, by definition. `validateResearchPublicationContent()` and
`pnpm research:validate` scan every publication's title, summary, findings, and section bodies
against `BANNED_CLAIM_PATTERNS` (definitive, world's most, most websites, industry standard,
comprehensive global study, peer-reviewed, academic study, scientific consensus, sensational
phrasing) and block publication if any match — an automated backstop, not a replacement for
editorial review (§159).
