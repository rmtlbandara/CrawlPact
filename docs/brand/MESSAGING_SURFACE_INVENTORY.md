# Messaging Surface Inventory

**Level 1 document (Current authoritative).** A complete inventory of every public,
authenticated, admin, and technical surface that carries product-descriptive or claim-bearing
copy, assessed against `docs/brand/BRAND_POSITIONING_AND_MESSAGING_SYSTEM.md`,
`docs/brand/CLAIMS_AND_MESSAGING_GUIDE.md`, and `docs/brand/VOICE_AND_STYLE_GUIDE.md`. Established
Phase 2, 2026-08-03, from direct inspection of every listed file (three parallel research passes,
each reading source files directly — no wording below is inferred).

Action vocabulary: `retain` (no change needed) · `minor-correction` (small wording fix) ·
`rewrite` (larger revision) · `centralise` (move to `apps/web/src/config/brand.ts`) · `remove` ·
`defer-phase-NN` (real gap, out of Phase 2's scope) · `verification-blocked`.

Claim classification: `verified-capability` · `verified-boundary` · `supported-inference` ·
`planned-capability` · `prohibited-claim` · `verification-blocked` (see
`docs/brand/CLAIMS_AND_MESSAGING_GUIDE.md`).

## Summary

Every public, authenticated, and admin surface inspected is already honest: **zero prohibited
claims, zero fabricated proof, zero stale-tagline occurrences in live product copy** were found
across all three research passes. The corrective work in Phase 2 is narrow: centralising
currently-duplicated brand strings into `apps/web/src/config/brand.ts`, a small number of
`minor-correction` wording fixes, one real cross-document conflict (SRS §2.3 vs. the new brand
system), and several completeness gaps that are correctly deferred rather than fixed here.
